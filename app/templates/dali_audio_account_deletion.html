Dali Audio

Request deletion of your Dali Audio account and data.

Use this page if you cannot access the deletion controls in Dali Audio. Dalifin LLC will verify ownership without asking for your password or recovery code.

Send a deletion request

Email {{ contact_email }} with the subject Dali Audio account and data deletion request. Include the email address associated with a registered account, if one exists, and a brief description of whether you used registered or accountless access. Do not email your password, recovery code, purchase token, or private document content.

What the request covers

  • Your Dali Audio account or accountless identity held by Dalifin.
  • Server-side Dali Audio projects, source scripts, generated audio, job state, preferences, and product access associated with that identity.
  • Deletion or anonymization of account-linked operational records where they are not required for security, payment, fraud-prevention, deletion proof, or legal obligations.

Files stored only on your phone cannot be removed remotely. Delete them in the app, clear Dali Audio storage in device settings, or uninstall the app.

Verification and timing

Dalifin may ask for limited information needed to verify that you control the account or installation. Verified requests are ordinarily completed within 30 days. We will explain any limited records that must be retained and confirm when the request is complete.

Purchases and subscriptions

Deleting Dali Audio data does not cancel a store subscription or reverse a purchase. Manage or cancel subscriptions separately in Google Play or the store that processed the purchase.

Read the Dali Audio Privacy Policy.
