Daligo account deletion

Request deletion of your Daligo account and data.

Daligo is provided by Dalifin LLC. You can delete your account in the app or submit a request here if you no longer have access to the app.

Request deletion by email

Email {{ contact_email }} from the email address registered to your Daligo account. Use the subject Daligo account deletion request and state that you want your Daligo account and associated data deleted.

We may ask you to verify control of the registered email address before processing the request. Do not send your password, payment-card details, or identity documents by email.

We will acknowledge the request and normally complete verified requests within 30 days. We will contact you if additional time is required by law.

Delete in the Daligo app

If you can sign in, open Settings, select Account, and choose Delete Account. Follow the confirmation steps shown in the app.

Account deletion is permanent. Export anything you want to keep before submitting the request.

Data covered by deletion

Deletion removes the Daligo account and account-associated server data, including profile information, trip membership, messages, shared location history, tracks, photos, contributions, and subscription-entitlement records, subject to the exceptions below.

Content copied to another member's device through nearby sharing may remain on that device until the recipient deletes it. Content another user created is not deleted as part of your request.

Limited retention and subscriptions

Dalifin may retain narrowly limited records when required for legal, accounting, security, fraud-prevention, dispute-resolution, or enforcement purposes. Retained data is restricted to those purposes and deleted when the applicable retention requirement ends. See the Daligo Privacy Policy for more information.

Deleting a Daligo account does not cancel a subscription managed by Google Play or Apple. Cancel it separately in your Google Play subscriptions or Apple subscriptions settings to prevent future renewals.
